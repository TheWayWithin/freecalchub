Understanding Compound Interest

Compound interest is interest earned on both the initial principal and the accumulated interest. It creates a "snowball effect," making it a vital concept for long-term investing. The basic formula is $A = P(1 + r/n)^{nt}$, but our calculator handles complexities like regular contributions.

Factors Affecting Growth

Several key factors influence how much your investment grows: Time Horizon (longer is better), Interest Rate (higher is faster), Contributions (regular additions boost growth), and Compounding Frequency (more often is slightly better). Consider these when planning.
