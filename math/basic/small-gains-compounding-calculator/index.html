How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter Your Starting Balance: Input the initial amount you're starting with (e.g., $10,000).
  2. Select Compounding Frequency: Choose how often returns compound. Daily is common for trading strategies, while monthly suits typical investments. For trading, select "252 trading days/year" to exclude weekends.
  3. Set Your Average Return: Enter your expected average return per period as a percentage. For example, 0.2 means 0.2% per day.
  4. Add Optional Deposits: If you plan to add money regularly, enter the deposit amount per period.
  5. Click Calculate: View your projected balance at 1, 3, 5, 10, 20, and 30 years, plus a growth chart showing the compounding curve.

The Power of Small, Consistent Gains

Most people underestimate how dramatically small gains compound over time. A seemingly modest 0.2% daily return doesn't sound impressive, but over years it creates extraordinary wealth. This is the core principle behind successful long-term investing and trading strategies.

Why Small Gains Beat Big Wins

  • Consistency beats volatility: A steady 0.2% daily gain compounds more reliably than occasional 10% wins mixed with losses.
  • Time is your multiplier: The longer you stay invested, the more powerful compounding becomes. Year 10 growth dwarfs Year 1.
  • Psychology matters: Small, achievable targets are easier to maintain than swinging for the fences.

The Math Behind It

For pure compounding, the formula is:

Final = Principal × (1 + rate)periods

With regular deposits, each contribution also compounds for its remaining time. The key insight: as periods increase, growth becomes exponential, not linear.

Real-World Perspective

Important: While this calculator shows mathematical possibilities, consistently achieving high daily or weekly returns for decades is extremely difficult. Markets are volatile, strategies fail, and drawdowns happen. Use realistic assumptions and understand that these projections represent best-case scenarios, not guarantees.
