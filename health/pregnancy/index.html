Due dates are typically calculated from your last menstrual period (LMP) or conception date, adding 280 days (40 weeks) from LMP. Our upcoming Due Date Calculator will provide accurate estimates, though only about 5% of babies are born exactly on their due date. Use our date difference calculators for precise timeline planning.

Online pregnancy calculators provide helpful estimates based on standard medical calculations, but they should complement, not replace, medical care. Always confirm important dates and milestones with your healthcare provider. Our calculators follow established medical guidelines for accuracy.

Beyond due dates, you may need nutritional calculations for proper pregnancy nutrition using nutrition calculators, weight gain tracking, appointment scheduling with date tools, and age-related pregnancy planning tools.

Pregnancy weeks are counted from the first day of your last menstrual period. The first trimester is weeks 1-12, second trimester is weeks 13-26, and third trimester is weeks 27-40. Our date difference calculators help track these important milestones.

Yes, pregnancy requires increased calories, protein, vitamins, and minerals. Our nutrition calculators can help estimate daily caloric needs and macro requirements, though always consult your healthcare provider for personalized nutritional guidance during pregnancy.

Use our date and time calculators to schedule regular prenatal visits, track ultrasound appointments, and plan for important milestones. Countdown calculators help you anticipate upcoming appointments and your due date.

Yes, all our pregnancy calculators perform calculations locally in your browser without storing or transmitting your personal health information. Your pregnancy data remains completely private. Review our privacy policy for complete details.

Maternal age affects pregnancy planning and risk assessment. Use our age calculators to determine exact age at conception or delivery, which helps healthcare providers assess pregnancy-related factors and plan appropriate care.

Healthy weight gain depends on your pre-pregnancy BMI. Generally, normal weight women should gain 25-35 pounds, underweight women 28-40 pounds, and overweight women 15-25 pounds. Use our weight management tools and nutrition calculators for guidance.
