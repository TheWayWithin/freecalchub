Understanding Units of Length

Units of length are used to measure the distance between two points. Different systems have developed historically, with the metric and imperial systems being the most common today.

The Metric System (SI Units)

The metric system is a decimal-based system used globally. Key units include:

  • Kilometer (km): 1 km = 1,000 meters. Used for long distances.
  • Meter (m): The base unit of length in the SI system.
  • Centimeter (cm): 1 cm = 0.01 meters (100 cm = 1 m). Used for smaller measurements.
  • Millimeter (mm): 1 mm = 0.001 meters (1,000 mm = 1 m). Used for very small measurements.

The Imperial/US Customary System

This system is primarily used in the United States. Key units include:

  • Mile (mi): Used for long distances. 1 mile = 1,760 yards = 5,280 feet.
  • Yard (yd): 1 yard = 3 feet.
  • Foot (ft): 1 foot = 12 inches.
  • Inch (in): The smallest common unit in this system.
