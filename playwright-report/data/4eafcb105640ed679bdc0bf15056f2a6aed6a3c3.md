Historical inflation averages 2-3% annually. Our calculator includes an inflation adjustment option to show both nominal and real (inflation-adjusted) target amounts, helping you set realistic goals that maintain purchasing power.
    - heading "What's the difference between monthly and lump-sum investing for goals?" [level=3]:
      - button "What's the difference between monthly and lump-sum investing for goals?"
    - region:
      - paragraph: Monthly investing (dollar-cost averaging) spreads risk by investing regularly regardless of market conditions, making it ideal for steady income earners. Lump-sum investing potentially captures more growth if markets trend upward, but requires having large amounts available upfront. Most people benefit from monthly contributions as it builds discipline, reduces timing risk, and makes large goals more achievable through smaller, manageable payments.

Consider your tax bracket, account types, and withdrawal timing when setting goals. Our calculator includes tax adjustment options to provide after-tax projections for more accurate planning.
